How to Play

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look around
  • Space Jump · double-tap to fly (creative)
  • Shift Sprint
  • Ctrl / C Crouch
  • Left click hold to break / attack
  • Right click Place block · eat food
  • Middle click Pick block
  • 1–9 / Wheel Select hotbar slot
  • E Inventory · F Crafting
  • Q Drop held item
  • G Swap creative / survival
  • H or F3 Debug info
  • Esc Pause menu

Touch / Tablet

  • Left joystick Move — push to the edge to sprint
  • Drag anywhere on the right to look
  • Tap the look area to break the targeted block
  • BREAK hold to mine · PLACE to build
  • JUMP · double-tap for flight in creative
  • CROUCH toggles sneaking
  • BAG inventory · CRAFT recipes · ☰ pause
  • Tap a hotbar slot to select it

Getting started

  1. Punch a Timber Log from a tree.
  2. Open crafting (F) and make Planks, then Sticks.
  3. Craft a Workbench and place it to unlock the full recipe list.
  4. Craft a Timber Pickaxe and mine stone → cobblestone.
  5. Craft stone tools, then a Furnace and Torches.
  6. Smelt Iron Ore into ingots for iron gear, then hunt Crystal Ore deep underground.
  7. Build a shelter before night — Night Grumbles hunt in the dark.

Creatures

  • Meadow Hopper — peaceful, drops raw cuts
  • Ridge Tusker — peaceful, sturdier
  • Cave Glimmer — glows underground, drops crystal shards
  • Night Grumble — hostile at night, burns at sunrise
  • Stone Spikeling — fast cave hostile
